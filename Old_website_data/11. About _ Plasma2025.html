ABOUT PLASMA 2025

PLASMA 2025

It is with great pride and anticipation that we welcome you to the 40th National Symposium on Plasma Science and Technology for Sustainable Future (Plasma 2025), the nation’s largest and most distinguished gathering dedicated to the field of plasma science, engineering, and technology. As Chairman of this milestone event, I am delighted to invite researchers, industry leaders, innovators, and policymakers to join us from 27–29 December 2025 in IIT Tirupati and IISER Tirupati, for what promises to be a truly transformative experience.

 

Plasma, the fourth state of matter, offers a unique composition of ions, electrons, neutrals, photons, and reactive species that has enabled breakthroughs across semiconductor fabrication, materials processing, fusion energy, nanotechnology, and advanced propulsion. In recent years, the emergence of near-room-temperature plasma has opened exciting possibilities in biomedicine, environmental remediation, food processing, and sustainable agriculture. Plasma science is increasingly becoming a central pillar in developing sustainable and high-impact technologies.

​

Plasma 2025 will serve as a vital platform to explore these developments through a rich program of keynote lectures, technical sessions, panel discussions, and interactive workshops. Topics will span fusion energy, additive manufacturing, plasma diagnostics, surface engineering, and applications in sustainability. With participation from academia, government, and industry, the symposium is designed not only to showcase the latest research but also to foster strategic collaborations and policy dialogue for the future of plasma-aided technologies.

​

The local organizing committee extends a warm welcome to all participants. We encourage you to engage deeply, share your insights, and take full advantage of this opportunity to shape the direction of plasma science and its role in building a better, more sustainable world. We look forward to seeing you in Tirupati this December.

ABOUT IIT TIRUPATI

Indian Institute of Technology Tirupati (IIT Tirupati) is an autonomous institution established by the Government of India as part of the prestigious IIT system. Founded in 2015 and located in the culturally rich state of Andhra Pradesh, IIT Tirupati has rapidly emerged as a center of excellence in technical education, research, and innovation. The institute offers cutting-edge undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs across disciplines including engineering, sciences, and humanities. With a strong emphasis on interdisciplinary research, IIT Tirupati is actively involved in addressing global challenges in energy, materials, environment, infrastructure, and emerging technologies.

​

Known for its vibrant academic community and state-of-the-art facilities, IIT Tirupati fosters strong industry-academic collaborations and encourages entrepreneurial initiatives among students and faculty. The institute also places a strong focus on sustainability, evident in its green campus initiatives and research in clean technologies. In addition to its academic and research pursuits, IIT Tirupati actively engages in national and international collaborations to promote knowledge exchange and translational research. The institute regularly hosts conferences, workshops, and symposiums that bring together leading scientists, engineers, and scholars from around the world. Its dedicated research centers—such as those focused on advanced materials, sustainable infrastructure, AI and data science, and renewable energy—serve as incubators for cutting-edge innovation. With a mission to nurture technically skilled and socially responsible leaders, IIT Tirupati is steadily building a legacy of excellence in education, research, and societal impact.

​

As a rapidly growing hub of innovation and intellectual exchange, IIT Tirupati continues to contribute to India’s technological advancement and global research landscape.

ABOUT IISER TIRUPATI

Indian Institute of Science Education and Research Tirupati (IISER Tirupati) is an autonomous premier institute established by the Government of India in 2015 under the Ministry of Education. As part of the prestigious IISER network, it is dedicated to promoting world-class education and cutting-edge research in the basic sciences. Located in the temple town of Tirupati, Andhra Pradesh, IISER Tirupati offers integrated B.S.-M.S. programs and Ph.D. programs in disciplines including biology, chemistry, physics, mathematics, and earth and climate sciences. The institute is known for its strong emphasis on a research-driven curriculum, hands-on laboratory training, and interdisciplinary learning.

​

IISER Tirupati is rapidly developing as a hub for high-impact scientific research. Its faculty are engaged in pioneering work in areas such as molecular biology, quantum physics, nanoscience, atmospheric chemistry, ecological conservation, and data science. The institute also encourages undergraduate students to undertake independent research projects under the guidance of faculty mentors. With state-of-the-art laboratories, an expanding research ecosystem, and a vibrant academic environment, IISER Tirupati fosters curiosity, creativity, and scientific rigor. The institute actively collaborates with national and international research organizations and is committed to developing future generations of scientists and innovators.

​

In addition to academic and research excellence, IISER Tirupati places a strong emphasis on outreach, societal engagement, and sustainability. The institute regularly organizes science camps, public lectures, and workshops to promote scientific awareness among school and college students, especially in rural areas. Its campus development aligns with green principles, aiming to create an eco-friendly environment that supports both biodiversity and innovation. Through its holistic approach to education and its commitment to inclusivity and scientific responsibility, IISER Tirupati is shaping a new generation of researchers equipped to tackle global challenges.
